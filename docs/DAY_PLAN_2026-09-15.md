### A3 — optional analytical scenario screen, before expanding CAD

Question: is the nominal shield advantage over a painted box stable under the
specific assumption changes already illustrated in the repository?

Proposed files, not yet implemented: `analysis/matched_control_sensitivity.py`,
`analysis/tests/test_matched_control_sensitivity.py`, and a short results section
in `analysis/thermal_bias_results.md`. Reuse `Variant`,
`solve_surface_temperature()` and `shield_air_preheat()`; do not fork the solver
or alter the defaults, committed day/night tables, or existing figures.

- Evaluate the eight combinations of V1 solar factor {0.18, 0.30}, convection
  multiplier {1.4, 1.0}, and calm preheat {1.2, 2.4 K}. Values come from the
  existing `sensitivity_block()`, not measured bounds or probability distributions.
- Use three specified cases: day (G=1000 W/m², wind=0.5 m/s, sky=10 °C),
  nominal night (G=0, wind=0, sky=10 °C), and warmer-sky night (G=0, wind=0,
  sky=29 °C), all at air=30 °C. Night preheat retains the existing G/1000 factor.
  There are 24 paired V0P/V1 cases; duplicate night predictions are meaningful
  controls, not additional independent evidence.
- Export inputs, source references, both signed biases and
  `abs(bias_V0P) - abs(bias_V1)` in °C. Positive means V1 is closer to ambient;
  “cooler” alone is not “more accurate” when bias may be negative.
- Tests cover nominal agreement with the existing solver, no mutation of base
  variants, zero-solar removal of absorptance/preheat effects, and a control in
  which identical variants have zero contrast. Do not assert every case favors V1.
- Write outputs only to a requested new location and retain commands and input
  identity. Report sign reversals and the implicated assumptions. This finite
  screen supplies no confidence interval, joint uncertainty bound, probability
  of superiority, or as-built model-agreement tolerance.

The review already found one reversal: nominal advantage +1.4771 °C becomes
−1.5531 °C when all three illustrated V1 perturbations are combined. A3 packages
the bounded comparison and controls; it is not an open-ended search for a
desired sign. Its decision output is which assumptions require measurement or
tighter justification before a hardware preference, not an automatic CAD go/no-go.

## First physical experiment — conditional, not scheduled by this plan

Use [COLOCATION_PROTOCOL.md](COLOCATION_PROTOCOL.md) as the draft authority.
Hypothesis: in the prospectively chosen illuminated/low-wind regime, V1 has
lower absolute reference-relative temperature bias than V0P; report night
behavior separately. Register the wind stratum before comparative observations.

Setup: actual dark baseline V0, identical-geometry painted V0P, and passive V1
where available, with an independently characterized reference at matched height.
No apparatus or calibration is verified yet. Hold sensor treatment, exposure
and heat-load accounting fixed or record the confounding changes. Prefer
simultaneous comparison; if only one box is available, the owner must approve
paired successive campaigns and their weather/time limitation before acquisition.

Measure signed bias, MAE/RMSE and paired availability by registered exposure
regime, alongside solar/wind, sky notes and interventions. The existing intake
is single sensor/reference per CSV: a multi-variant campaign needs separately
identified exports on a shared declared clock/window, preserving raw originals
and documented mappings. Successful intake of each file alone does not prove
cross-variant pairing or authenticate the campaign.

The pilot's numeric **data-readiness** criteria remain 1,440 planned one-minute
slots over 24 h, ≥90% finite paired samples, ≥120 illuminated paired samples
(solar ≥200 W/m²), ≥120 low-solar paired samples (≤5 W/m²), and declared paired
U95 ≤0.5 °C supported by records. A scientific superiority/agreement criterion
still requires the application tolerance, as-built predictions and appropriate
uncertainty treatment fixed before observations. Do not invent that missing
number from the 1.4771 °C analytical example or treat 1,440 correlated rows as
independent replicates.

Timebox: one 24-hour acquisition plus a first review within two working days
after authorized access; weather or failed checks may require a prospectively
approved extension. Start only after the actual readiness gates close. Stop or
pivot when calibration/permissions cannot support the proposed comparison;
retain inadequate coverage as incomplete. If acceptable paired evidence fails
the predeclared advantage criterion, report it and reconsider the shield's
complexity rather than adding CAD/FEA to rescue the expected outcome. One pilot
does not establish seasonal accuracy.
